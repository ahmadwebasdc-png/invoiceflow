FAQ

Good to know.

Where is my data stored? +
This starter stores data in JSON files on the server machine. Back up the server/data folder regularly and do not expose the unauthenticated app to the public internet.
Can I download a PDF? +
Yes. Open a saved invoice preview and use Download PDF. The PDF library is loaded from a CDN, so internet access is needed for that feature.
Does it send invoices or collect payments? +
No. This app creates invoices and tracks payment status locally. Email delivery, payment processing, authentication, and cloud sync are not included.
